Kickoff Legends

From academy trialist to legend. Train your player, earn a contract, play the decisive moments of every match and fill your trophy cabinet.

What you do

  • Train — shooting, dribbling, passing, sprinting, defending and free-kick drills on the training ground raise your attributes.
  • Get signed — reach 60 overall and real clubs from 13 countries and 3 divisions each come calling.
  • Play your moments — the match runs on the ticker; when the ball comes to you, you take control: attack, counter, defend, take the free kick or the penalty.
  • Grow — match ratings, playstyles, transfers, international call-ups, cups and continental nights.
  • Live the life — money, lifestyle, brand deals, social media and the phone that never stops ringing.
