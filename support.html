Frequently asked

Where is my career saved?

On your iPhone. To keep it safe or move it to another phone, create a free account in Settings → Account → CREATE ACCOUNT; your careers are then saved online and LOG IN restores them.

How do I delete my account?

In the game: Settings → Account → DELETE ACCOUNT → DELETE FOREVER. This permanently deletes the account and everything stored online with it (players, saves, leaderboard entries, friends, gameplay counts and reports). Without an account, ask us here to delete your leaderboard entries. See the privacy policy.

Someone has an offensive name.

Open their profile from the leaderboard and tap REPORT or BLOCK. We review every report within 24 hours.

There is no sound.

Check the volume and the in-game Settings (sound effects, crowd, music). After a phone call or another app playing audio, touch the screen once to wake the game's sound.

Why do I see ads?

Kickoff Legends is free. A short video plays after every 10th finished training session or match — never in the middle of play. Settings → NO ADS FOREVER removes ads for good; RESTORE PURCHASES is next to it. You can change your ad privacy choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

The game feels slow on my phone.

Settings → Graphics → MED or LOW lowers the resolution and shadows.
