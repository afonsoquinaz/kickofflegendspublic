Frequently asked

Where is my career saved?

On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes the career; there is no account or cloud save.

There is no sound.

Check the volume and the in-game Settings (sound effects, crowd, music). After a phone call or another app playing audio, touch the screen once to wake the game's sound.

Why do I see ads?

Kickoff Legends is free. A short video plays after every 8th training session or match — never in the middle of play. You can change your ad privacy choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

The game feels slow on my phone.

Settings → Graphics → MED or LOW lowers the resolution and shadows.
